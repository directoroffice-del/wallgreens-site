Formwork Systems · Form-on H20 Range

The global benchmark of timber beam

The H20 is the world's standard timber formwork beam — 200 mm deep, light enough for one worker to carry, stiff enough to span far enough that prop counts drop and slab cycles shorten. Wallgreens is the authorised India distributor for Form-on GmbH. Every beam is made in Austria to EN 13377, from PEFC-certified timber, with tensile testing on the flanges and machine stress-graded chords. We hold stock in Mumbai and Morbi and supply pan-India, on outright purchase.

Three Variants — Same Structural Capacity

3-ply web
Form-on smartBEAM 20N
approx. 4.4 kg/m
Three-ply web construction with optimised finger-jointing. Roughly 10% lighter than the oP, which cuts transport cost and makes long lengths easier to handle by hand.
Particle board web
Form-on smartBEAM 20P
approx. 4.4 kg/m
Wood-composite particle board web with the smartBEAM cross-section — combines the pressboard's uniform load-path with the smartBEAM chord geometry. Well suited for wall and column formwork where lighter handling matters.
Particle board web
Form-on timberBEAM oP
approx. 4.9 kg/m
Wood-composite pressboard web, one continuous material along the full length. Capacity does not vary from one end of the beam to the other. Preferred for slab as the primary and secondary formwork driver.

Key Features

  • Consistent along the whole length. The homogeneous web means load capacity does not drop at a joint or a knot. What the beam carries at mid-span, it carries at every span.
  • Every flange tested. Chords are machine stress-graded and 100% tensile tested before the beam is assembled — capacity is verified, not assumed.
  • EN 13377 certified. The European standard for timber formwork beams. Where a project specification calls for an EN-certified beam, this is the compliant answer.
  • Fewer props than squared timber. Higher load capacity at lower weight means wider primary beam and prop spacings — less material on site, fewer trips up the slab, faster stripping.
  • One beam for walls, columns and slabs. The same section works as a soldier behind wall panels, as a column clamp, and as a primary or secondary slab beam. One item on the BOQ instead of three.
  • PEFC-certified timber. Sourced from sustainably managed forests — relevant on projects with green-building or ESG reporting requirements.
  • Outright purchase, pan-India. Supplied through Wallgreens as the authorised India distributor for Form-on. Cleaning, repair and return logistics available on request.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an H20 timber beam?

A 200 mm deep timber I-beam — the international standard section for wall, column and slab formwork.

Who manufactures the H20 beams Wallgreens supplies?

Form-on GmbH, Amstetten, Austria. Wallgreens is the authorised India distributor.

What is the difference between timberBEAM oP and smartBEAM 20N?

Web material and weight only; structural capacity is identical. Both meet EN 13377 with M 5 kNm, Q 11 kN, E×J 450 kNm².

What load can an H20 beam carry?

M 5 kNm, Q 11 kN, E×J 450 kNm². Use the span calculator above for spacings at your slab thickness.

How do I purchase H20 beams in India?

H20 is available for outright purchase only, pan-India — through Wallgreens as the authorised India distributor for Form-on. Cleaning, repair and return logistics available on request.

What lengths are available?

Ten lengths from 1.80 m to 5.90 m. Five held ex-stock in India (2.45, 2.90, 3.60, 4.50, 4.90 m). The rest are on indent from Austria.
